# Gold Seed — estudo da descida 0.1

Preview branch: journey-v51-network-preview.
Entry: journey-v52/descent.html; V52 tools > Camadas > Abrir estudo da descida.

Five replaceable concept plates JGS-ART-001–005 are delivered as 1024px WebP images, with the preserved V51 Golden Seed extracted into an independent transparent WebP actor. PNG concept originals are preserved.

Route: descent-route.json. Explicit user advance or vertical pointer swipe changes view; transitions finish in WAIT and cannot autoplay. Background zoom/roll and lateral seed displacement approximate descent and wind. Final view stops at the trail; Patita and Nice Party remain future scene integration.

Shared responsive-preview.js and responsive-stage.css were copied unchanged from jaguar/piano-stage-v0.1. Auto/Portrait/Landscape are developer framing modes; real touch-first devices use Auto.

Current rendering is two crossfading raster plates plus a separate seed. It does not implement calibrated 3D camera pitch, collisions, leaf occlusion, portals, physical wind or through-branch geometry. Those require foreground cutouts/spatial geometry. Generated art provides visual continuity guidance, not a single measured 3D world.

Animation, swipe and WAIT produce no pedagogical evidence, score, Session or routing authority.
