# JAGUAR H14.26 — WHY · Independent Task Blueprints (S1/S2)

## Scope, status and methodology
This is a **read-only authoring specification**, not an activated assessment. It continues H14.25 trilingual contextual review using the original S1/S2 experience seeds, H14.5, H14.6 and `data/learning/skills/why.json`. It creates six scoped plans: S1-origin and S2-origin × EN/ES/PT, each with three separately defined pedagogical dimensions. There are 18 blueprint *slots*, NOT 18 approved exercises, Attempts or Evidence records.

## Three evidence demands — not interchangeable
**Question-function** must independently establish WHY as a causal question, distinct from HOW as manner. The existing Dependency Head Probe (a question about the verb/predicate modified by WHY) can be a teaching reference but is **not standalone proof** of causal-intention understanding. The independent assessment prompt remains null pending authoring.

**Reason-answer (local)** needs a learner's independent contextual justification to the fully grounded S1 cheese-preference question or S2 shared-preparation question. Model responses and contrast alternatives are kept teacher-only. Replaying a Corpus Speech Card, selecting a visible reference, or answering a grammatical reformulation cannot grant credit.

**Reason-answer (transfer)** needs a second independent answer in the approved alternate situation, using a distinct causal reason. A learner may encounter that scene before completing local proof; completion of all requirements—not route order—determines Green Pass, only under an existing, subsequently authorized contract.

## Proposals and boundaries
- `WHY-S1-S2-DRAFT`: S1 source reason `pairs-with-salmon`, transfer in S2 with `sharing-preparation`.
- `WHY-S2-S1-DRAFT`: S2 source reason `sharing-preparation`, transfer in S1 with `pairs-with-salmon`.
- S2's **dual-role ambiguity** is resolved only by explicit selection intent, origin Experience, scope, and occurrence. Never infer from visiting S2.
- Scope ownership is learner × skill × language × origin Experience. PATITA observations remain separate; 0/4 contextual adequacy is not 0/3 contractual progress.
- Only PRESENT/AFFIRMATIVE source references are grounded for these exploratory WHY cases. PAST, FUTURE, other MODES and PERIOD are not authorized through this blueprint. PERIOD remains `GEAR-06-PERIOD-GAP`.
- Spanish `prepararla` refers to `la cena`; Portuguese `prepará-lo` refers to `o jantar`. The text and meaning still require final human linguistic review.
