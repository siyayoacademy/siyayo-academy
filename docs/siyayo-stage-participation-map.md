# SIYAYO Stage Participation Map

**Status:** v1.0 — Piano Stage branch  
**Purpose:** decide what earns the right to participate on the trilingual stage, why it belongs, which actor presents it, and where pedagogical authority remains.

## Admission rule

A source enters the stage only when we can answer seven questions:

1. **What is it?**
2. **Where does it come from?**
3. **What does the learner do with it?**
4. **Which actors present or respond to it?**
5. **Which languages are genuinely available?**
6. **Is this exploration, opportunity, candidate evidence, or canonical authority?**
7. **Where is the experience trying to go?**

The stage never upgrades presentation into Evidence by itself.

## Four-layer reading

```text
CONTENT
what is being played

+ ACTIVITY
what the learner is doing

+ ACTOR ORCHESTRATION
Piano / Pianinho / Frondosa

+ PEDAGOGICAL AUTHORITY
explore / WAIT / Evidence / GREEN / Resume / Advance
```

## Actor interpretation

**Piano Plano** is primarily accompaniment / structural or harmonic anchor.  
**Pianinho Mágico** is primarily melody / interactive agent.  
**Frondosa** is primarily perceptual guide / semantic surface.

These are tendencies, not prisons. Experience bindings choose the active capabilities.

## Key coherence rules

- Exact semantic identity wins over shared note identity.
- Solfege may resonate by note; semantic collections resonate by exact item.
- Language is a lens, not the collection itself.
- Tripiano is a sequence EN → WAIT → ES → WAIT → PT.
- Frondosa may speak a note, chord, word, example, mark a pulse, provide a semantic hint, or remain silent.
- GREEN, Evidence, Resume and Advance remain canonical responsibilities.
- A selected route is not an executed navigation.
