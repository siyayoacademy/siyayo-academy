# JAGUAR — HOW TO DO / HOW TO RECORD / HOW TO SHOW — Independent pedagogical access

Status: **normative architectural rule** for SIYAYO ACADEMY / Master VIP / Verb Explorer. This document restates the previously approved requirement; it does **not** introduce a new teaching or assessment contract.

## Non-negotiable invariant

**A contextual practice or pedagogical module must NOT depend on an active assessment, NICK, or GREEN PASS in order to appear.**

- **HOW TO SHOW** displays available, grounded learning activities according to Experience, Question Word, selected language and other relevant GEAR dimensions. Its visibility must be driven by pedagogical capability, not by evaluator/session availability.
- **HOW TO DO** enables free study, listening, exploration, movement and interaction **with or without NICK**. Changing language, Experience, QW or GEAR settings must refresh all corresponding pedagogical surfaces coherently.
- **HOW TO RECORD**: PATITA observes navigation and exploratory interactions without assigning assessment credit. **Only an authorized assessment contract** may evaluate admissible evidence and award progress/credit/GREEN PASS within the appropriate NICK, skill, language, Experience and scope.

## State and authority boundaries

1. Display state is distinct from assessment state. A saved evaluation in ES must never suppress the EN/PT pedagogical module. Distinguish DISPLAY language and ASSESSMENT language in the Learning Trail.
2. Navigation (NEXT/PREVIOUS, language, QW, word type, tense, mode, perspective) must re-render relevant displays on entry and return **without requiring a redundant second click on the same QW**.
3. Completed and partial contractual progress remains isolated and persistent under its existing authorized identity/scope. Neither switching language nor browsing freely may reset it or award new credit.
4. No fabricated answers, implied evaluation, implicit enrollment, duplicate attempts or invented GREEN PASS from simply viewing, listening or clicking exploratory material.
5. When no pedagogical corpus is authored, show a truthful, localized unavailability/authoring state, not a fake assessment or a blank page. The ten kinds of words and remaining QWs are distinct dimensions of the Master VIP, not subordinate to QW assessment.
6. Preserve existing modules, maps, checkpoints, evidence provenance and previous CI gates. Only change a confirmed integration gap.

## Regression acceptance (minimum)

- Fresh NICK and no NICK: navigate S1→S2→S3→S4, select WHY, alternate EN→ES→PT→EN, return S3. Pedagogical WHY S3/S4 surfaces must appear whenever WHY and matching Experience are selected.
- Same steps with an authorized, partially completed WHY assessment: preserve separate progress per language and show an appropriate active evaluation only where scope permits. Exploration never awards credit.
- Already confirmed 3/3: return and explore freely with retained trail, no restart or duplication.
- Check PATITA observation, contract evidence, Learning Trail, Word Path, and speakers independently.
- Apply the same invariants to WHICH / WHAT / WHERE and subsequently the other QWs, NOUNS, ADJECTIVES, VERBS and other word types; no blanket assumption that content/contracts are already authored.
